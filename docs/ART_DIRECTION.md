# Art direction — OBSIDIAN No. 01

**World:** black glass / volcanic stone / amber light / precise typography / controlled motion.
**First impression:** a carefully photographed fragrance campaign that is also a usable product page.
**Signature moment:** the single sticky "The Object" sequence on desktop (O0 → O3). Everything else stays quiet.

## Decisions (made before code)

1. **Audience** — prospective clients of 13:33; in three seconds they should read "campaign", not "template".
2. **References** (owner's screen recordings) — principles only: the product is the hero, scroll reveals the
   object, macro detail, very little interface chrome. No layout copied.
3. **Palette** — one dark family, one light editorial surface, one accent used for meaning
   (eyebrows, active storyboard note, focus ring). Never decorative gold.
4. **Type** — one display serif with character + one readable sans; a fixed six-step scale.
5. **Motion** — one hero loop, one scroll-linked sequence, 640 ms entrances. All of it can be paused.

## Typography

Chosen by rendering "OBSIDIAN" and "Leave an impression." in nine serif candidates and four sans candidates
(`source/scripts/font_specimen.py`):

- **Cormorant Garamond** (variable, OFL) — display and the bottle print. Strong character, elegant at 144 px,
  stable hairlines. Bodoni Moda lost its hairlines at small sizes; Gloock was too heavy; Fraunces' wonky
  forms fought the product. Lining numerals forced with `font-variant-numeric: lining-nums`.
- **Hanken Grotesk** (variable, OFL) — controls, labels and body. Warm, neutral, excellent at 13 px uppercase.

Both are self-hosted Latin subsets (`public/fonts`, ≈72 KB total), preloaded, `font-display: swap`,
licences alongside.

| Step | Size |
| --- | --- |
| display | `clamp(52px … 144px)` |
| h2 | `clamp(48px … 100px)` (48 px at 390 px wide, ≈92 px at 1440) |
| h3 | `clamp(36px … 52px)` |
| lead | 18–21 px |
| body | 17 px |
| label | 13 px, 0.16 em tracking, uppercase |

## Layout

- Max content width 1440 px; gutters 20 / 32 / 64 px (phone / tablet / ≥1200).
- 4-column phone grid, 12-column desktop grid (≥900 px). 8 px spacing scale; identical section padding
  (`--section-y`) everywhere.
- Section pattern: eyebrow (accent label) → serif title → short lead.
- Buttons 54 px high, 3 px radius, exactly one filled primary per view.

## Canonical product

One procedural Blender model (`source/blender/obsidian_scene.py → build_bottle`) is the source for every
render, the hero loop and the GLB used by the web viewer, so identity cannot drift.

| Part | Normalised value |
| --- | --- |
| Body | 1.00 w × 0.55 d × 1.45 h, corner radius 0.08 |
| Glass walls / thick base | 0.05 / 0.20 |
| Neck | 0.10 h (glass, unioned with body) |
| Cap | Ø 0.45, 0.35 h including a 0.022 brass ring |
| Total height | 1.90 |

Materials: smoked glass (IOR 1.52, warm-tinted absorption), dark amber liquid, satin black cap
(roughness 0.30), warm brass (metallic, roughness 0.24), screen-print in warm off-white (`#ECE5D8`).

**Typography on the bottle** is a deterministic texture (`source/scripts/make_label.py`) in the same two
typefaces — never generated by an AI model, never an HTML overlay. 50 mL and 100 mL variants differ only
in the printed volume. The geometry is identical for both sizes (concept simplification, noted in the
storyboard).

### Lighting rig (studio)

Warm rim/key behind right · thin warm strip left · soft fill from the far left (outside the front face's
mirror angle) · faint top · narrow "sweep" strip for the moving shoulder highlight · warm back-glow on a
seamless sweep · a dedicated print light linked only to the label. Photographic flags are implemented with
Cycles light linking (cap excluded from fill/sweep/top; floor excluded from key/strip).

### Rejected during look development

Grey translucent glass (absorption too low) · silver-looking cap (softbox reflections) · fill reflection
covering the label · visible floor horizon · light leaks on the floor · "melted" shoulder refractions in
the macro shots (fixed by lowering the camera to 3–4°).

## Imagery for scent chapters

Generated still lifes (Higgsfield → Nano Banana Pro, 2400×1792; first drafts in Figma) graded in
`build-media.mjs` (saturation 0.86, slight warm channel balance) so they share the campaign palette.
No bottle, no text, no logos in generated media — the product only ever comes from Blender.
