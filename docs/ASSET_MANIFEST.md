Generation dates: **2026-10-05** (stills, scent keyframes) and **2026-10-06** (films). Masters live outside public assets
(`source/renders/`, `source/frames/`, `source/generated/` — git-ignored) and are reproducible from the

T1 and its 9:16 twin were first blocked by a preset recommendation and resubmitted with

(saturation 0.9, brightness 0.98, channel balance 1.02/1.00/0.96).
